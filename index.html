What this page will not do

No clinic named.

Nothing here ranks, recommends or names a provider. There is no list, and we are not paid by anyone on it.

No treatment recommended.

The arithmetic says nothing about what any treatment does, how many sessions you need, or which one is right for you. Session counts here are examples of how one travel bill divides.

No price compared.

Your quote is divided, never matched against another price. A two-price comparison is switched off until a lawyer has read it, and this build has no other price in it to compare with.
